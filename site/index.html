Working thesis

The family is a governance system, not a pile of agents.

Use deterministic controls when facts are deterministic. Use AI when interpretation, synthesis, investigation, or bounded autonomous work adds value. Keep identity, policy, evidence, authorization, and durable state outside agent memory.
