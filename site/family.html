Core

Binder

Family manifests, namespaces, schemas, identity, sync/rollback, version compatibility, evidence references, and ownership boundaries.

Govern

Basecoat

Engineering policy, reusable instructions, AI-SDLC controls, secure defaults, guidance distribution, control mappings, and governance orchestration.

Experience

Sheen

Design tokens, semantic tokens, themes, accessibility, interaction patterns, design guidance, and UI conformance.

Verify

Adhesion

Tests and evaluations across functional, security, accessibility, performance, policy, architecture, model, prompt, and agent behavior.

Catalog

Batchbook

Enterprise catalog of governed applications. Each Batch carries identity, graph, composition, controls, evidence, lifecycle, and ownership.

Discover

Snitch

Collectors and observations. Finds unknown, unmanaged, changed, or contradictory assets without pretending discovery equals canonical truth.

Understand

Teardown

Reverse-engineers repositories, applications, containers, SBOMs, IaC, AI components, topology, and Genealogy.

Coordinate

Crosslink

Call trees, on-call, responders, escalation paths, acknowledgements, incident bridges, business owners, and vendor coordination.

Prove

Proof

Evidence, provenance, Stamps, attestations, CoA generation, confidence, assurance, integrity, and evidence validity.

Product boundary rule

Do not turn every concept into another product.

Stencil, Formula, Blueprint, Reclaim, Stamp, CoA, Genealogy, Notary/Witness, and deployment classes may remain primitives or services unless independent runtime ownership creates real value.
