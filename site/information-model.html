Core information architecture

Batchbook → Batch → Swatch

The catalog is not a flat CMDB. It is a graph of governed applications, their composition, ownership, history, controls, evidence, and operational relationships.

Enterprise catalogBatchbook
↓
One governed applicationBatch
↓
Compact profile / manifestSwatch

Batch

A complete governed application unit: identity, ownership, classifications, graph, architecture, approved composition, controls, evaluations, responders, evidence, deployments, and lifecycle.

Swatch

The concise human- and machine-readable representation of a Batch: name, purpose, owner, criticality, classifications, profiles, key technologies, and references.

Deck

A durable grouping of Batches, such as a portfolio, business domain, CUI boundary, or manufacturing application estate.

Palette

A curated or dynamic selection of Batches, such as “all applications using model X” or “production apps with expired exceptions.”
