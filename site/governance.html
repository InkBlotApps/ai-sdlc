Core idea

Governance makes intent operable

Governance is broader than law or compliance. It establishes direction, boundaries, authority, accountability, feedback, and the mechanisms that translate expectations into behavior.

Governance actors

Authority / governing body, policy owner, communicator/enabler, control owner/enforcer, validator, assurance/auditor, and governed actor.

Autonomous agents

Automation may execute delegated governance authority; it does not create that authority. Agent actions must trace back to an approved policy/control and accountable owner.

Exceptions

Exceptions are first-class: owner, approver, reason, compensating controls, expiration, evidence, and review.

Reversibility

Prefer reversible controls until confidence and authority justify irreversible action: warn, quarantine, read-only, rollback, archive, restore, then delete when appropriate.
